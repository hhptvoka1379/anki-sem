# xtest-img method notes (CHIM ch.1, 40 records)
- Images opened: 8 full pages at 200 dpi (PDF 12, 13, 14, 15, 27, 256, 257, 258), displayed whole; 1 extra 300 dpi crop of PDF 15 (Q10/Q11) to check a doubtful option.
- Resolution that worked: 200 dpi full page (downscaled to ~1414x2000 in view) was enough for stems, options, subscripts/superscripts (A₁, 10⁻³¹, H₄P₂O₇, N⁺, K⁺) and the printed key tables. No column crops were needed; the 300 dpi crop only confirmed what the 200 dpi view showed. Solution pages (256-258) are low-contrast scans but still readable.
- Unsure records: none for text. Worth a human look: CHIM-C01-NCOM-010 (options C and E both printed "Ne", confirmed on crop; key D = H, not affected), CHIM-C01-COMM-002 (stem says 10⁻³¹..10⁻²⁹, reasoning says 10⁻²⁷..10⁻²⁵ kg; both copied as printed).

- ocr_flags: empty for all 40 records (nothing unreadable). figure: null for all (no question needs a figure).
- Not taken from handwriting: circled letters, ticks, and fill-ins were ignored; every key comes from the RISPOSTE pages (COMM letters + reasoning from PDF 256-257, NCOM keys 1-15 from the table on PDF 257, COMP words from PDF 258).
- Notes: COMP page 27 shows 16 items; only Q1-15 were extracted as asked. COMM Q9 reasoning continues from PDF 256 to 257 and was joined. "ad libidum" in COMM Q6 reasoning is the book's spelling.
- Wall time: about 89 s (includes viewing 8 pages and writing the records).
